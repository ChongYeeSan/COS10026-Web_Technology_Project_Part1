Site Supervisor (RENEWABLE ENERGY)

Reference Number: RE026

Salary: RM3,500 - RM5,000 per month

Position Reports To: Project Manager

Description:

Supervise daily on-site solar engineering installations and civil project milestones. Coordinates subcontractor workflows, enforces workplace health and safety compliance, and reports site performance metrics back to the central tracking network.

Key Responsibilities:

  • On-Sites Supervision: Oversee day-to-day civil and electrical installation activities of commercial solar photovoltaic (PV) systems
  • Safety & Compliance: Enforce workplace health, safety, and environmental protection regulations on-site to maintain zero-incident operations.
  • Progress Logging: Record Physical installation milestones and submit field metrics to the corporate operations and maintenance tracking system.

Essential and Preferable Requirements:

  1. Education: Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
  2. Experience: Minimum of 2 years of hands-on experience in on-site construction supervision or solar PV installations.
  3. Certification: Valid CIDB Green Card for site safety compliance in Malaysia.
  4. Skills: Strong familiarity with NET Energy Metering (NEM) guidelines and local grid connection regulations.

Solar Sales Consultant

Reference Number: SG104

Salary: RM3,500 - RM4,500 per month

Position Reports To: Sales Manager

Description:

You will transform company-provided leads into successful closings, owning the end-to-end customer journey from first contact to final handover as a solution consultant for Ecopower.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Effectively manage and convert company generated leads that has been assigned daily.
  • Build strong customer relationships and ensure satisfaction through follow-ups.
  • Take full ownership of sales process from lead assignment to final customer handover.

Essential and Preferable Requirements

  1. Education: Bachelor's Degree in Business, Engineering or any related field is preferable.
  2. Experience in Consultative Selling, including identifying customer needs and recommend tailored solutions.
  3. Proactive and goal-oriented with ability to meet and exceed sales targets.
  4. Fluent in multiple languages like Bahasa Melayu, English, and Chinese.
